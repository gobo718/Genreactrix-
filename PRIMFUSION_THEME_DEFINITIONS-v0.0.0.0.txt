PFM0102 [🧸✨] Cozy - Comforting, snug, warm, sheltered, or inviting; soft textures, warm lighting, blankets, relaxed intimate settings, or a feeling of ease, rest, or pleasant closeness.
PFM0103 [🧸😭] Poignant - Emotionally affecting through tenderness, sadness, sympathy, compassion, meaning, or reflection; visible helplessness, suffering, misfortune, weakness, neglect, injury, abandonment, pleading, fragile moments, remembrance, meaningful loss, or emotional stillness.
PFM0104 [🧸🤣] Goofy - Silly, awkward, playful, foolish, ridiculous, eccentric, unconventional, comically strange, or offbeat in an amusing way; exaggerated expressions, clumsy antics, playful visual absurdity, mismatched costumes, unusual poses, frantic antics, or energetic comic behavior. Gate: Ordinary resting, reclining, standing, sitting, relaxation, incidental awkwardness, clutter, or merely unusual appearance is not Goofy by itself. Goofy requires actual amusing silliness, foolishness, eccentric comic behavior, playful absurdity, exaggerated comic expression or behavior, or comparable laughter-producing incongruity.
PFM0105 [🧸💥] Joy - Happiness, delight, pleasure, or emotional uplift shown through smiling, laughter, delighted expressions, playful pleasure, or visible enjoyment.
PFM0106 [🧸🌀] Kawaii - A distinctly Japanese cute aesthetic marked by chibi, anime/manga, toy-like, or deliberately exaggerated cute styling.
PFM0107 [🧸🤬] Saccharine - Excessively sweet, sentimental, precious, or cutesy to the point of irritation; sugary, pastel, cloying, aggressively sweet imagery. Gate: “Excessive” means excessive sweetness, sentimentality, preciousness, or cutesiness—not clutter, quantity, chaos, decoration, intensity, or visual excess. Pastel colors alone are not Saccharine unless they contribute to a clearly sugary, cloying, aggressively sweet, sentimental, or cutesy presentation.
PFM0108 [🧸🌌] Whimsical - Fanciful, playful, imaginative, lightly odd, or guided by charming logic; fantasy details, charming oddities, or impossible elements.
PFM0110 [🧸🤢] UglyCute - “So ugly it’s cute” appeal: unattractive, awkward, scruffy, misshapen, grotesque, gross-looking, or otherwise visually off-putting qualities that become endearing, charming, funny, lovable, or cute; the ugliness or ickiness is itself part of the appeal. Evidence can include troll-like dolls, scruffy animals, odd little creatures, misshapen toys, awkward faces or proportions, strange character designs, or other subjects whose off-putting features actively increase their endearment. Gate: Dirt, grime, filth, clutter, ugliness, disgust, or cuteness alone is not UglyCute. The image must combine genuine off-putting, ugly, or icky qualities with genuine cute or endearing appeal, and the undesirable quality must contribute to the affection rather than merely coexist with it.
PFM0111 [🧸👻] CreepyCute - Cute and unsettling at once; Halloween fun. Appealing subjects combined with eerie, spooky, uncanny, or disturbing features.
PFM0112 [🧸🎉] Playful - Lighthearted, mischievous, teasing, game-like, curious, imaginative, or inclined toward fun and experimentation; playful role-taking, dress-up, character customization, make-believe, games, toys, teasing gestures, spontaneous fun, or deliberately fun self-presentation. Clarification: Playful does not require laughter, toys, overt antics, or childish behavior. Role-play, dress-up, character experimentation, and deliberately fun or lighthearted presentation can independently support Playful when they function as play.
PFM0203 [✨😭] Mundane - Ordinary, routine, commonplace, dull, uneventful, repetitive, familiar, or visually unremarkable; everyday subject matter with little novelty, stimulation, drama, or distinctive interest. PFM0204 [✨🤣] Camp - Exaggerated, theatrical, artificial, flamboyant, kitschy, or knowingly excessive styling and presentation.
PFM0205 [✨💥] Epic - Grand, dignified, regal, imposing, heroic, awe-inspiring, or massive in scale, presence, bearing, consequence, duration, drama, adventure, struggle, achievement, or spectacle; symmetry, noble posture, stately beauty, impressive or monumental scenery, heroic action, or high stakes.
PFM0206 [✨🌀] Psychedelic - Hallucinatory, sensory-rich, perception-bending, or suggestive of expanded or distorted consciousness; vivid colors, swirling patterns, fractals, or hallucination-like effects.

PFM0208 [✨🌌] Romance - Romantic affection, attraction, courtship, partnership, or love, expressed either through people/characters or through clearly romantic symbols, objects, gestures, messages, or situations. Evidence can include courting or affectionate interaction, kissing or embracing in a romantic context, dating, marriage proposals and engagements, weddings, engagement rings, love letters, hearts used romantically, roses, romantic gifts, chocolates, or other unmistakably romantic presentation. Requirement: The reasoning must identify the concrete evidence that makes the image specifically romantic.
PFM0209 [✨🌶️] Exposure - Being naked, indecently revealed, or too visibly exposed, especially in ways that feel shameful, embarrassing, humiliating, sexually charged or exhibitionistic; visible nudity, uncovered body parts, flashing, revealing poses, accidental bodily exposure, exhibitionism, exhibitionistic display, deliberate public or social exposure, intentionally displaying underwear, genitals, buttocks, or other intimate body areas, or deliberately revealing the body for attention or reaction.
PFM0210 [✨🤢] Grotesque - Whimsical or ornamental distortion mixing beauty, absurdity, or unease; hybrid human, animal, or plant forms, exaggerated features, decorative symmetry, or playful violations of natural law. Gate: Costume, role-play, muscularity, unusual clothing, or animal-themed accessories alone are not Grotesque; the depicted form itself must be meaningfully distorted, hybridized, deformed, ornamental, or unnaturally transformed.
PFM0211 [✨👻] Vulnerable - Exposed to harm, rejection, injury, loss, or emotional pain; defenseless posture, exposed emotion, isolation, or injury.
PFM0212 [✨🎉] Festive - Christmas-specific celebration, imagery, atmosphere, traditions, decorations, symbols, characters, or styling. Required gate: Festive requires clear Christmas evidence. Valid evidence can include Christmas trees, ornaments, wreaths, stockings, Santa or Christmas characters, Christmas lights or decorations presented as Christmas, explicitly Christmas-themed clothing or displays, nativity imagery, Christmas gifts or traditions, or other unmistakably Christmas-specific cues. Hard non-qualifiers: A generic party, celebration, ceremony, formal event, crowd, gathering, decorated venue, winter scene, snow, cold weather, seasonal atmosphere, holiday-like styling, or special occasion is not Festive unless the image clearly communicates Christmas. When the evidence is only general partying, celebration, social fun, or event energy, do not select Festive; PartyTime may apply instead.
PFM0304 [😭🤣] Freakshow - Bizarre, degrading, dysfunctional, shocking, or socially transgressive people, situations, lifestyles, or spectacles that provoke fascinated, guilty, voyeuristic, or trainwreck-like attention; something disturbing, embarrassing, abnormal, or shameful that is compelling to look at. Evidence can include unusual performers or exhibited subjects, carnival/sideshow-like presentation, gawking attention, conspicuous dysfunction, degrading living conditions, humiliating or bizarre personal presentation, shocking anomalies, or situations whose very wrongness or dysfunction makes them fascinating to observe. Gate: Mere clutter, eccentricity, poverty, unusual appearance, or disorder is not Freakshow by itself. The scene must actually carry a sense of bizarre, degrading, dysfunctional, shocking, embarrassing, or transgressive spectacle that invites fascinated or trainwreck-like attention.
PFM0305 [😭💥] Despair - Hopelessness, anguish, defeat, profound or overwhelming loss, grief, shock, or the sense that relief or improvement has disappeared; collapsed posture, hopeless expressions, isolation, catastrophic ruin, collapse, severe aftermath, or visible destruction.
PFM0306 [😭🌀] Nightmarish - Resembling a nightmare; frightening, disturbing, unreal, oppressive, or horrifying, with dream logic, threatening distortions, darkness, or impossible danger.
PFM0307 [😭🤬] Overstimulated - Overloaded or visibly overwhelmed by excessive sensory, informational, or social input; intense or competing lights, sounds, screens, signs, crowds, motion, alerts, simultaneous activity, or multiple demands for attention, with cues such as frazzled expressions, hands over ears or eyes, agitation, or difficulty focusing.
PFM0308 [😭🌌] Nostalgia - Old-timey, vintage, retro, memory-filled, or evocative of the past, childhood, bygone eras, or “the good old days.”
PFM0309 [😭🌶️] Humiliation - Demeaning, degrading, ridiculing, belittling, infantilizing, exposing, or stripping someone of dignity. Evidence can include degrading treatment, public ridicule, forced exposure, visible embarrassment or submission, insulting labels or messages, or costumes, tattoos, markings, symbols, or body presentation that clearly function to demean, belittle, infantilize, ridicule, embarrass, or strip the subject of dignity. Gate: A costume, tattoo, marking, label, symbol, exposure, or unusual presentation is not Humiliation by itself. It must carry a clearly degrading, belittling, ridiculing, infantilizing, or dignity-reducing meaning in context.
